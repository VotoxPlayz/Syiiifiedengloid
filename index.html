How Syii Translation Works

Syii is not a simple 1:1 cipher. It is an expressive dialect where words like soid and sooweh rely heavily on sentence context, surrounding words, and established phrasing.

View Known Translation Rules & Systems
  • Known Phrases: Multi-word idioms like i rrrrr ("I don't know / denial of responsibility") are resolved before individual words.
  • The Soid System: soid dynamically resolves to song, sick, Six, sense, sing, see, Singh, or scene depending on preceding words.
  • The Sooweh System: Resolves as an exclamation replacement ("hell") or dynamically points to previously established nouns in context.
  • -istan Suffix: Sentence-ending -istan is stripped and interpreted as terminal punctuation.
  • -oid Suffix System: Detects -ing, -ly, or on/out usages based on word position and stems.
  • High density preservation: Words with ambiguous or uncertain rules are highlighted gently rather than replaced arbitrarily.
